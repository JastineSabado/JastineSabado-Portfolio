These roles strengthened my ability to manage teams, coordinate projects, and communicate effectively with diverse stakeholders. I am driven to continuously learn and apply technology to solve real-world problems, with a growing focus on combining UI/UX thinking and data analytics to build impactful digital solutions.

I strive to bridge technology, design, and data to deliver solutions that are both functional and impactful.
